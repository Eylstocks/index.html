About us

Nugaal Hotel is a hotel in the center of Garowe, 50 meters from the main square. We welcome business travelers and families.

Choose a Single Room, a Twin Room or a Suite. Every room has a private bathroom and free Wi-Fi. The hotel has backup power and daily housekeeping.

Our restaurant and coffee shop is open to everyone, and hotel guests can use our conference room.
