NUGAAL HOTEL - HOW TO CHANGE A PHOTO

1. Take or choose the new photo (landscape, taken in daylight, bed made).
2. Rename it to EXACTLY the same name as the photo you want to replace.
3. Upload it into the 'images' folder and choose 'Replace' when asked.
